#4471 was three problems, not one

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-11-01 · 0 days ago

#4471 was marked known: one issue, one fix queued. Its reports were merged by subject line (decision 0001), so nobody had to read the body. Read the bodies, and three problems come out, each with its own fix. Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October. That closed two of them.

The three problems

problemwhat reporters seeticketstate
parserDay and month swapped: “Booked for 2 March. The invite says 3 February.”#1fixed: parse dd/mm for eu locales
hintThe same swap, only through the booking-v3 form, which shows MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants#2won’t fix: booking-v3 retired
one-day moveThe date moves by one day, not a month: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”#3open
  • parser: the shared parser read every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates came out flipped.
  • hint: people typed what the form’s hint told them, month first, and the form read it the tenant’s way. Fixing the parser left it in place. Retiring the form ends it.
  • one-day move: these are the reports the table above footnotes as read as a one-day move. The desk files them under parser by tenant, so they sit inside the biggest row. They look like a time zone, not a parser, and neither change on 5 October touches them.
#4471 is closed, but not every report in it is solved. The one-day moves have their own ticket, #3, and no fix yet. Send new one-day reports there, not to #4471.

Why the merge hid it

  • Subject lines don’t name the cause. “Date is wrong” covers all three problems.
  • Intake tags guess from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. The “tagged as” column above shows how often they disagree with the cause.
  • One ticket with a count reads as one bug. A count says how loud, not how many.

Next time

  • Split a big ticket by cause, not subject, before marking it known: try the triage explorer.
  • Read a sample of bodies in every big ticket. A handful of reports was enough to find a third bug.
