  • hint: tenants on booking-v3 show the hint MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Same flip, different bug: fixing the parser leaves it in place. Only reports sent through the form.

Why the merge hid it

  • Subject lines don’t name the cause. The same subject (“Date is wrong”) covers all three.
  • Intake tags guess from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. The “tagged as” column above shows how often they disagree with the cause.
  • One ticket with a count reads as one bug. A count says how loud, not how many.
